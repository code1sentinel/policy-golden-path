      The golden path

      Policy development follows one path: each stage has to carry the intent of the one before it. PolicyGP checks each stage, and the links between them.

      1. Policy intent What the organization commits to Your input: a policy file or intent
      2. Control requirement What the control demands Your input: an OSCAL catalog
      3. Implementation statement How it is met today Checked against the policy intent and control
      4. Risk statement Where practice falls short, and what it could cost Checked against the control and policy it fails
      5. Recommendation How to get back on the path Checked against the cause in the risk statement

      Best practices at each stage

      A practice counts as adopted when it scores or more with no outstanding area for improvement, and partly adopted from . These describe how a statement is written; they are not a pass or fail.

      Scores reflect how a statement is written, not whether it is true. The Awareness, Assessment and Adoption approach follows CSA's Internet Hygiene Portal; this tool is not affiliated with CSA.
