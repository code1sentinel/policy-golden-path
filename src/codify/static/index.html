AI drafting

Off

Off unless you turn it on. Codify still drafts with rules. With AI on, you can also ask a model to draft a clause's controls, with your own API key or with Ollama on this machine (no key). Every AI draft is scored like any other, marked as drafted by AI, and stays a draft until you review it.

What is sent. One clause per request: its text, its section heading and Codify's rule drafts for it. It goes straight from this browser to the provider you choose, under your account and that provider's terms. It does not pass through Codify, and your key stays in this browser.
Do not send classified or sensitive text. Use AI drafting only for policy text your organisation allows you to send to this provider. If in doubt, leave it off: the rule drafts need no AI.
