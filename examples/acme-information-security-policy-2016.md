# Acme Agency Information Security Policy

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Version | 3.0 |
| Owner | Chief Information Officer |
| Approved | Management Committee, 14 March 2016 |
| Classification | For internal use |

*Acme Agency is fictional. This policy was written for Codify as an example of a legacy policy.*

## 1. Purpose

1.1 The Agency is committed to protecting the confidentiality, integrity and availability of its information assets.

1.2 This policy sets out the minimum security requirements that all staff are expected to follow.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This policy applies to all employees, contractors, interns and third parties who access Agency information or IT systems, wherever they are located.

2.2 This policy covers all Agency IT systems, including servers, desktops, laptops, mobile devices and cloud services.

## 3. Definitions

3.1 "Information asset" means any data, system or device owned or managed by the Agency.

3.2 "Privileged account" means an account with administrative rights to a system.

3.3 "Removable media" means USB drives, external hard disks, CDs and similar devices.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

4.1 The Chief Information Officer (CIO) is accountable for this policy and for approving exceptions to it.

4.2 The IT Department is responsible for implementing the technical controls in this policy.

4.3 Line managers shall ensure that their staff are aware of and comply with this policy.

4.4 All users are responsible for protecting the information they handle.

## 5. Access Control

5.1 Access to Agency systems shall be granted on a need-to-know basis and approved by the user's line manager.

5.2 User accounts shall be reviewed regularly and access that is no longer required shall be removed as appropriate.

5.3 The IT Department shall disable the accounts of staff who leave the Agency, transfer to another department, or are on long-term leave.

5.4 Privileged accounts shall only be used for administrative tasks and shall not be used for email or browsing the internet.

5.5 Shared or generic accounts should be avoided where possible.

## 6. Passwords

6.1 Passwords shall be at least 8 characters long, contain a mix of letters and numbers, and be changed every 90 days.

6.2 Users shall not share their passwords, write them down, or reuse their last 5 passwords.

6.3 Users must change their passwords every 90 days.

6.4 Accounts shall be locked after 5 failed login attempts.

## 7. Protection Against Malicious Software

7.1 Symantec Endpoint Protection shall be installed on all desktops and servers and kept up to date.

7.2 Users must not disable or tamper with anti-virus software.

7.3 Users must not open suspicious email attachments, click unknown links, or download software from untrusted websites, etc.

## 8. Patch Management

8.1 Security patches shall be applied to all systems in a timely manner.

8.2 The IT Department should test patches before deployment where practical.

## 9. Backup and Recovery

9.1 The IT Department shall back up all servers nightly using Veritas Backup Exec, and backup tapes shall be stored off-site.

9.2 Restoration of backups shall be tested periodically.

## 10. Logging and Monitoring

10.1 Security logs, such as firewall and server logs, should be reviewed as needed.

10.2 System logs shall be retained for an appropriate period.

## 11. Email and Internet Use

11.1 Agency email and internet access are provided for business purposes. Limited personal use is permitted.

11.2 Users must not send confidential information to personal email accounts.

## 12. Removable Media

12.1 The use of USB drives is discouraged.

12.2 Confidential information copied to removable media shall be encrypted.

## 13. Third Parties

13.1 Third parties shall comply with this policy.

13.2 Vendors with access to Agency information shall sign a non-disclosure agreement before access is granted.

## 14. Incident Reporting

14.1 Users shall report any suspected security incident to the IT Helpdesk immediately.

14.2 The IT Department will investigate reported incidents and take appropriate action.

## 15. Physical Security

15.1 The server room shall be kept locked at all times, and access shall be restricted to authorised IT staff.

15.2 Visitors shall be escorted at all times.

## 16. Exceptions

16.1 Any exception to this policy must be approved in writing by the CIO.

## 17. Compliance

17.1 Non-compliance with this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

17.2 Staff are encouraged to be vigilant and to promote a culture of security.

## 18. Policy Review

18.1 This policy shall be reviewed annually by the CIO.
